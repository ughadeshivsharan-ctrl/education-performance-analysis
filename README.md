# 📊 360 Education Business Analytics Platform

## 📌 Project Overview

360 Education Business Analytics Platform is an end-to-end data analytics project developed to analyze the performance of an education business.

The project covers the complete business journey from lead generation and admissions to student placements, revenue, and fee collection.

The main objective of this project is to help management make data-driven decisions using interactive dashboards and business insights.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze lead generation and lead conversion
- Track student admissions
- Analyze branch and course performance
- Monitor student placement performance
- Analyze placement by company and course
- Track revenue and fee collection
- Analyze payment status and payment modes
- Identify business trends and performance gaps

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- Power BI
- SQL
- Excel
- Python
- DAX

## 📊 Dashboard Pages

### 1. Executive Overview

Provides a high-level overview of the education business.

Key metrics include:

- Total Revenue
- Total Students
- Total Admissions
- Remaining Fees
- Placement Rate
- Lead Conversion %

### 2. Sales & Admission Analytics

Analyzes the sales and admission process.

Key analysis includes:

- Total Leads
- Converted Leads
- Lead Conversion %
- Total Calls
- Effective Calls
- Sales Funnel
- Converted Leads by Lead Source
- Branch-wise Lead Performance
- Employee Performance
- Admission Trend

### 3. Placement Analytics

Analyzes student placement performance.

Key analysis includes:

- Placed Students
- Placement Rate
- Average Placed Salary
- Placement Calls
- Active Placement Students
- Placement Rate by Course
- Branch-wise Placement
- Company-wise Placement
- Monthly Placement Trend

### 4. Fees & Financial Analytics

Analyzes the financial performance of the education business.

Key analysis includes:

- Total Revenue
- Total Collected Fees
- Fee Collection %
- Average Course Fee
- Pending Fees
- Monthly Revenue
- Monthly Fee Collection
- Branch-wise Revenue
- Payment Status
- Course-wise Revenue
- Payment Mode Analysis

Lead Generation → Lead Conversion → Admissions → Students → Placement → Revenue & Fee Collection
